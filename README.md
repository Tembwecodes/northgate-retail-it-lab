# Northgate Retail Group — IT Support Lab

A self-built IT support environment for a fictional small retail business, **Northgate Retail Group**, built to demonstrate the practical skills expected of a Tier 1/Tier 2 support technician: helpdesk operations, identity and access management, scripting and automation, remote support, technical documentation, structured troubleshooting, and cloud administration.

This is a personal project, built solo, from a clean environment. It is not real employment. The company, its users, and its incidents are simulated, but the tools, configurations, and troubleshooting are real.

## The scenario

Northgate Retail Group is a small retail business with a Head Office (IT, Finance, HR) and two store locations (Store Ops – North Branch, Store Ops – South Branch). Everything in this repo, the AD domain, the helpdesk categories, the knowledge base, and the M365 tenant, is built around this one consistent company so the projects read as a single coherent support environment rather than seven disconnected tutorials.

## Background

Built following CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+ certification, as practical, hands-on evidence to sit alongside them. Documented as I went, including the mistakes, since that's closer to what a real support role actually looks like than a clean success story would be.
